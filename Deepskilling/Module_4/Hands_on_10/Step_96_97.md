# Microservices Decomposition

| Service Name         | Responsibility                      | Endpoints Owned    | Database         |
|----------------------|-------------------------------------|--------------------|------------------|
| Course Service       | Course CRUD, Department CRUD        | /api/courses       | courses.db       |
| Student Service      | Student CRUD, Enrollment            | /api/students      | students.db      |
| Auth Service         | Login, Registration, JWT Validation | /api/auth          | auth.db          |
| Notification Service | Email Confirmations                 | /api/notifications | notifications.db |

## Why Split?

Each service owns its own business logic and database.

Course Service:
- Manage courses
- Manage departments

Student Service:
- Manage students
- Manage enrollments

Auth Service:
- User authentication
- JWT token validation

Notification Service:
- Email notifications
